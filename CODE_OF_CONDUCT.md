# Code of conduct

This project's standard is short, and it applies to everyone in its issues,
pull requests and discussions, maintainers included.

- **Be straightforward and assume competence.** Say what you mean, and read
  others as meaning what they say.
- **Disagreement about a measurement is resolved by measuring**, not by
  seniority, volume or persistence.
- **Criticise the work, not the person.** Harassment, personal attacks,
  discriminatory language and unwelcome sexual attention are not acceptable.
- **Respect privacy.** Do not post anyone's personal information without their
  consent.
- **No promotion schemes.** Star, fork or follow exchanges, and unrelated
  advertising, are closed as off-topic.

## Reporting

Email <support@tekmyra.ai>. Reports are read by the maintainers and kept
confidential. Security problems follow SECURITY.md instead.

## What maintainers do

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove comments, close issues and pull requests,
and block accounts that break this code. Where it is safe to, they will say
which part of the code was broken and why.
